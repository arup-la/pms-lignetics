Channel Owner Guide
For: Margaret Graese · Luann Lafreniere · John Crass · Tony Woods · Arup Basu (Rolling Stock)
1
Logging in
Open the app file in any browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari). Enter your email address and personal password — both will be shared with you at app roll-out. Your session stays active for 8 hours so you won't need to log in repeatedly during the day.
2
Updating your stage progress — Owner View tab
Click the 👤 Owner View tab. Select your name from the dropdown — your channels appear as tabs at the top. For each channel, work through the stage cards and:
  • Click a status button — Not started, In progress, or Complete
  • Enter your planned start and end dates (blue labels)
  • Enter your actual start and end dates once work is underway (green labels)
  • Add any notes in the free-text field at the bottom of each card
  • Use Mark N/A to exclude a stage that does not apply to your channel — it will be greyed out and excluded from your progress calculations
Click 💾 Save when done. Switch between channels using the tabs.
3
Stage 8 questionnaire — Stage 8 Questionnaire tab
Click the 📝 Stage 8 Questionnaire tab. Choose your preferred response mode using the toggle at the top:
  • All channels — fill in one set of answers covering all your channels. Click Apply to all channels to copy across, then switch to Per channel mode to add specific overrides
  • Per channel — answer each channel separately using the channel tabs
The questionnaire has 15 questions across 5 sections: Channel & Spend · Supplier Landscape · Contracts & Documentation · Spend Visibility & Process · Capacity & Readiness. Click 💾 Save at any time — you can return and continue later.
4
Printing or saving your channel summary
In the Owner View, scroll to the bottom of any channel and click 🖨 Print to open a formatted one-page summary in a new window — you can print to paper or save as PDF from the browser's print dialog. Click 📄 Save as PDF to download a self-contained file with your initials and a timestamp in the filename.
5
Other tabs available to you
  • 📋 Channels & Owners — quick reference showing all 15 channels, their owners, sub-categories, and live progress
  • 🔀 Process Flow — the full interactive 15-stage methodology, always available for reference
  • 📊 PM Dashboard — program-level view; you can view this but updates are made in your Owner View
💡
Need help? Contact Arup Basu at abasu@lignetics.com for any questions, access issues, or corrections to your channel information.
📊
Program Manager Guide
For: Arup Basu · Blaine Emery (PM full access)
1
PM Dashboard — program overview
The 📊 PM Dashboard is your home tab. It shows:
  • 🔔 Upcoming deadlines — click to expand; shows all stages due within 7 days across all channels with urgency badges. Click Send reminder to open a pre-drafted Outlook email to the relevant owner — review and send
  • Program metrics — overall completion %, stages complete, in progress, overdue, and RS handover date
  • Owner progress cards — click any card to jump directly to that owner's view
  • Stage tracker — click any owner row to expand their channel sub-rows; click any dot to cycle stage status (Not started → In progress → Complete)
  • Master Gantt — click to expand; 15 stage bars with completion %, slippage, and traffic lights 🟢 🟡 🔴
  • Channel Gantt — click to expand; 15 channel bars showing planned window (hollow) vs actual progress (solid fill)
2
Updating stage data on behalf of owners
Go to 👤 Owner View, select an owner from the dropdown, and update their channels exactly as the owner would. All changes are saved to the same local storage — click 💾 Save to commit. You can also click any dot directly in the PM Dashboard tracker to cycle an owner's stage status without leaving the dashboard.
3
Viewing Stage 8 questionnaire responses
Go to 📝 Stage 8 Questionnaire. Click the 📊 View all responses button (visible to PM only) to see a master table of all 15 channels with completion %. Click any row to expand and read the full answers for that channel inline. This view is read-only — answers can only be edited by the owner.
4
Exporting to Excel
Click ⬇ Export to Excel (.xlsx) on the PM Dashboard (in the Channel Gantt header). The file downloads with a date and time stamp in the filename and contains 5 tabs:
  • Stage Data — all 15 channels × 15 stages with status, dates, slippage, and notes
  • Master Gantt — program-level summary with traffic lights per stage
  • Gantt Chart — weekly colour-coded channel bars Feb–Oct 2026
  • Stage 8 Questionnaire — all owner responses, one row per question per channel
  • Legend — colour coding and symbol reference
5
Sending deadline reminders
The 🔔 Upcoming deadlines panel at the top of the PM Dashboard scans all channels for stages due within 7 days that are not yet complete. Click ✉ Send reminder next to any item to open a pre-drafted email in Outlook addressed to the relevant owner, with the stage details and urgency level pre-filled. Review the email and click Send. The button turns green once actioned.
6
Traffic light key
  • 🟢 Green — on track (≥80% complete or all applicable stages done)
  • 🟡 Amber — in progress / watch (≥40% complete or at least one stage active)
  • 🔴 Red — action needed (one or more stages past their planned end date)
  • ⚫ Grey — not yet started (future stage, no activity)
